Preach Law LLP is a Delhi-based professional services and legal practice providing legal consultancy, advisory, corporate support, taxation, intellectual property, accounting and compliance services.

WHO WE ARE

A modern
legal practice.

Preach Law LLP is a law and professional advisory firm based in Delhi. The firm brings together legal and professional expertise across a broad range of areas, serving individuals, businesses, companies, financial institutions, public-sector organisations and non-governmental organisations.

The firm's stated areas of practice include civil and criminal matters, commercial and corporate work, insolvency and bankruptcy, taxation, banking and finance, intellectual property, real estate, labour and industrial disputes, consumer matters, regulatory advisory and corporate compliance.

Preach Law also provides professional support in areas including company formation, corporate affairs, accounting, auditing, taxation, secretarial services, due diligence and related business advisory functions.

Established in February 2015 The firm was established by a group of experienced professionals who had previously worked together in the legal sector.
